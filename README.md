# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive HR Analytics Dashboard developed using Power BI, DAX, and Power Query to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, salary distribution, and employee retention trends.

## 📌 Project Description

This project focuses on analyzing HR employee data to identify attrition patterns across age groups, education backgrounds, salary slabs, job roles, and years at the company. The dashboard provides interactive visualizations to support data-driven HR decision-making.

## 💡 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall employee attrition and retention trends.
- Identify age groups with higher employee attrition.
- Analyze attrition across salary slabs and job roles.
- Understand the relationship between years at the company and attrition.
- Support HR teams in identifying areas for employee retention initiatives.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Employees | 1,470 |
| Employees Who Left | 237 |
| Attrition Rate | 16.1% |
| Average Age | 37 Years |
| Average Salary | 6.5K |
| Average Years at Company | 7.0 |

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Overall Attrition: 237 out of 1,470 employees left the organization, resulting in a 16.1% attrition rate.
- Age Group: Employees aged 26–35 recorded the highest attrition count (116).
- Salary Slab: Employees earning up to 5K recorded the highest attrition count (163).
- Job Role: Laboratory Technicians had the highest attrition count (62), followed by Sales Executives (57).
- Education: Life Sciences accounted for the largest share of attrition (38%).
- Gender: Male employees accounted for 140 attritions, compared with 79 female attritions in the gender visual.
